# PlantSense hackathon delivery

Reviewed 14 October 2026. This is a delivery target, not a claim that PlantSense is production ready or will win.

## Product promise and users

PlantSense helps a reliability engineer and production manager turn abnormal equipment observations into an evidence-backed maintenance decision, then verify the repair. A technician records what inspection established and what work was performed. AI gathers read-only context and explains uncertainty; people authorize consequential work.

The connected factory simulation explains dependencies and response timing. It is a separate reference scenario, never a substitute for operational evidence. Keep the colleague's automatic plant, Operators, machine records, case table and guide-first Memory.

## Industry-informed boundaries

These are design references, not compliance certification or a complete industrial integration specification.

- [ISO 17359:2018](https://www.iso.org/standard/71194.html) describes general procedures for a machinery condition-monitoring programme. Its public abstract does not validate this app's detector, envelopes or hydraulic assumptions. Preserve equipment identity, units, source revision, observation time, recorded evidence and diagnostic uncertainty.
- [NIST's Generative AI Profile](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) identifies risks including confabulation. Preserve source inspection, missing evidence, read-only model tools, human authority and evaluation of injected notes and conflicting observations. Output validation does not prove semantic correctness.
- [OSHA's hazardous-energy overview](https://www.osha.gov/control-hazardous-energy/) informs the distinction between software authorization and physical maintenance controls. App approval or a simulated stop never establishes isolation, a permit, trained personnel or safe equipment. Applicable site procedures and jurisdiction-specific requirements need qualified review before industrial use.
- Repair completion and recovery are different events. Operational recovery requires three distinct complete healthy windows after recorded completion. Browser scenario recovery is timer-based and remains outside verified operational memory.
- Configured capacity exposed, estimated output, measured production and financial assumptions remain separate. Monetary arithmetic stays in the existing Python engine, in integer paise.

## Delivery order and acceptance

1. **Connected case records:** freeze synthetic detection context; preserve historical gaps; link operational investigation, authorized work, recovery and matching memory. Scope assistant requests to the displayed evidence category. Keep retry payloads and keys stable.
2. **Live AI proof:** run investigator and assistant fixtures, including saved synthetic evidence, current-versus-historical distinction and injected notes. Failed or stale reports do not activate providers. Record actual usage, latency and limitations.
3. **Business and workflow demonstration:** show one complete maintenance decision and a later retrieval of confirmed repair memory. Use attributable historical data or explicitly authored isolated fixtures; describe neither as live plant measurements. Show uncertainty and failed recovery as supporting proof.
4. **Submission release:** when the user explicitly resumes deployment, validate cloud identity and shared persistence for the staff workflow before exposing it. The older anonymous cloud adapter does not complete that requirement. Run public acceptance, record the video, export the deck and check every submission link.

Defer new physics scenarios, inventory, ERP/CMMS/PLC adapters, broad scheduling and further 3D polish until these acceptance conditions pass. Cloud deployment remains paused. No additional screens are needed to prove the core promise.

## Data separation

New browser cases save their synthetic detection value/envelope and a bounded copy of affected equipment and line capacity. That snapshot survives sample eviction and later layout changes. Legacy cases without it show unavailable historical topology; no current topology is assigned retroactively.

The added dossier and scenario-history UI were removed at the user's request. The interface is restored to `bd31976`; further visual or navigation changes require approval. Scenario timers do not confirm a mechanical cause or enter operational repair memory.

Connected journey panels and matching-memory navigation are proposed work requiring design approval, not current restored UI behavior. Assistant requests with operational incident context cannot also carry browser simulation. Optional saved synthetic detection remains a typed, ownership-checked backend source, separate from current browser readings and authoritative plant state.
